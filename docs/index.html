Make room for every piece.

Drag all the pieces into the square. Fill every space, without overlaps or pieces sticking out.

You can move pieces as often as you like. From level 5, select a piece and tap Rotate, or double-tap the piece to turn it. If a piece needs more room, lift it back into the tray.

You can also tap a piece, then tap the board to place it. With a keyboard: select a piece, use arrow keys to position, R to rotate, Enter to place, and Escape to cancel.
